3  Preface

3.1 Welcome to Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625: Last-Minute Revision Guide

This comprehensive revision guide has been specifically designed to support students preparing for the Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 examinations (2026-2028 syllabus). Whether you are aiming for top grades or seeking to strengthen your understanding of fundamental physics concepts, this guide provides the essential tools you need for success.

3.1.1 Purpose of This Guide

The primary objective of this revision guide is to:

  • Consolidate your knowledge of all core and extended syllabus topics
  • Clarify common misconceptions that often lead to mistakes in examinations
  • Develop essential problem-solving skills through worked examples and practice questions
  • Build confidence through structured revision and targeted practice

3.1.2 How to Use This Guide

This guide is organized to maximize your revision efficiency:

  1. Core Content Chapters (Chapters 1-23): Each chapter covers a specific topic from the syllabus, including:
    • Clear learning objectives aligned with syllabus requirements
    • Concise explanations of key concepts
    • Common misconceptions and how to avoid them
    • Worked examples demonstrating problem-solving techniques
    • Practice questions at both Core and Extended levels
  2. Supporting Materials: The back matter includes:
    • Practical skills guidance for laboratory work
    • Essential mathematical and data analysis techniques
    • Examination strategies and time management tips
    • Comprehensive formula sheet for quick reference

3.1.3 Study Recommendations

To get the most from this revision guide:

  • Start early: Begin your revision well before the examination date
  • Active learning: Don’t just read—work through examples and attempt practice questions
  • Identify weaknesses: Use the practice questions to identify topics requiring additional study
  • Regular review: Revisit topics periodically to reinforce your understanding
  • Practical work: Ensure you understand the experimental techniques and can apply them to unfamiliar contexts

3.1.4 A Note on Success

Physics is not just about memorizing formulas—it’s about understanding the principles that govern our universe and applying that understanding to solve problems. This guide aims to develop both your conceptual understanding and your ability to apply physics principles in various contexts.

Remember: consistent effort, active engagement with the material, and regular practice are the keys to success.

We wish you the very best in your studies and examinations.
